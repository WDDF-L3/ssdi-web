COORDINATION & ACADEMICS

Keeping every course grounded in real outcomes

Day to day, my role is to make sure what happens inside the classroom actually prepares students for what waits outside it. That means regularly reviewing our curriculum with instructors, tracking how recent graduates are doing in the job market, and adjusting course content when a skill gap shows up.

We run a tight feedback loop between students, mentors and our careers desk, so issues get caught early rather than at graduation. If you ever have a question about how a program is structured, our academic team is always reachable.
